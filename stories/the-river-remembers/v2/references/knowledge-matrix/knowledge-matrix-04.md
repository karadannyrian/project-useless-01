### Sensory and Perceptual Constraints — Chapter 4 (Combat)

**During the ambush:**
- Alexandria's perception will narrow. Combat reduces the world to threat vectors. Her cataloging reflex may be suppressed by action.
- She will not have time to note small details about Hark's hands or face; she will see his movement, his blade, the bodies falling.
- She may experience adrenaline-driven time dilation, but not emotional introspection.
- The Void Step she uses will be automatic; she will not consciously name it. If she sees Hark move similarly, she will register the similarity after the fact, during the aftermath.

**During the aftermath:**
- She will catalog the dead, the survivors, the state of the caravan.
- She will observe Hark's behavior: standing apart, not speaking, not looking at the bodies.
- She will assess Vallus's state: shaking, possibly ashamed.
- She will note her own physical state: breathing, wounds if any, blade condition.
- She will not name feelings of fear, relief, gratitude, or shock. Somatic cues only.

### Chapter 4 Closing Constraint

Chapter 1: resonant pause. Chapter 2: abrupt cut. Chapter 3: in-transit close (watch). Chapter 4 must use a fifth variation. Options:

1. **Action-lingering close:** End on a somatic aftereffect—her hands still trembling slightly as she cleans her blade, though she does not name the tremor as fear.
2. **Abrupt cut:** End mid-thought as she catalogs the dead, a detail that snags.
3. **Earned seam:** *They rode on. The bodies stayed behind.* (Compressed, carries weight.)

Recommendation: **Action-lingering close.** The chapter is violent; the close should let the violence settle without resolving it. Her hands tremor. She notes the tremor, files it as *adrenaline aftereffect*, and does not name it further.
