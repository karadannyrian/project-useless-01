# Protagonist Knowledge Matrix — Chapter One

## Alexandria Whittier — POV Character (Chapter 1)

### What She Knows

**Her Identity:**
- She is Alexandria Whittier, Silencer First Oath, age 15
- She was surrendered to a foundling house at three days old
- She has no known family, no known bloodline
- She is Insensitive — confirmed by two Savant verifications
- She took the First Oath at age 10
- She is the Order's instrument, and this is her first solo deployment

**Her Training:**
- The Threefold Path: Shell, Needle, Eye
- Her Void Step is excellent; her Closing Strike is exceptional
- She can read frost-scars, tremors, and desiccation markers — the Debt-Read — though she treats it as a checklist
- She knows the rhythmic patterns of incantation across all five Gates
- Her Final Syllable success rate is 87% under combat stress
- She has never experienced a failure that mattered

**Her Mission:**
- Cover identity: junior escort-in-training, House Kellan
- Handler: Vara, retired placed Water-mage, operating from a scribe's shop in Salthaven
- Mission parameters: observe, catalog unmarked magical activity, do not engage
- This is a training exercise — routine long-term surveillance
- She reports to Vara; Vara forwards to regional Order branch

**The Town (pre-arrival briefing only):**
- Salthaven is a minor port in the southern Maelstrom League
- No Order branch within two days' sail
- No naval base, no noble houses of consequence
- The League is the Order's problem client — they train their own Edge specialists, they negotiate aggressively, they harbor escaped mages
- The Archivists have flagged unauthorized Water-mage healing signatures in the southern League
- Salthaven is one of several candidate locations
- She is a probe — if she finds evidence, the Order escalates

**The Order (what she believes):**
- The Order collects Sensitive children to protect them
- The Order trains mages to control their dangerous abilities
- The Order places mages in service to the equilibrium
- The Order is merciful, necessary, and true
- The Iron Rule is justice: no unmarked mages
- Silencers are the Order's final sanction, the debt that cannot be borrowed
- She is proud to be what she is, though she would not use the word *proud*

**What She Reads First in Any Environment:**
- Hands
- Exits
- Weapons and weight
- Faces (reluctantly)
- Small beautiful things (noticed, immediately suppressed)

### What She Does NOT Know

**About Her Parents:**
- She does not know her mother is alive
- She does not know her mother is Renne, the Water-mage healer who leads the River network
- She does not know her mother surrendered her at three days old with bandaged hands and a lullaby that made the air cold
- She does not know her father is alive
- She does not know her father is Damian Whiters, escaped Fire/Light mage, now operating as Hark
- She does not know either parent is in Salthaven
- She does not know she was born from love, not abandonment

**About the Order (what she does not know):**
- She does not know the Order executes children during "Controlled Escape Exercises"
- She does not know the Order buries files on escaped mages it is embarrassed by
- She does not know the Order's "mercy" includes Permanent Sedation for children deemed unsuitable
- She does not know the Order holds placed mages' families as hostages (as with Vyktor)
- She does not know her foundling house was an Order institution — that the Order raised her from the moment of her surrender
- She does not know the Savant who performed her Mirror reading (Ashkel) will one day speak her father's birth name in combat
- She does not know the Silencer she trained beside (Miren) will one day be sent to kill her mother

**About the Mission:**
- She does not know this is not a training exercise — it is a probe for a purge
- She does not know Vara has been a placed mage for decades and has her own complicated history with the Order
- She does not know the town of Salthaven is actively protecting an underground network
- She does not know the "unmarked magical activity" she is sent to map is her mother's healing work
- She does not know the "quiet mercenary with gloves" she will observe is her father

**About Herself:**
- She does not know she is capable of love
- She does not know she is capable of defiance
- She does not know her indoctrination is a framework, not an identity
- She does not know she was designed not to question — and that design is about to fail
- She does not know the word *daughter*

## Hark — NOT a POV Character in Chapter 1

Hark does not appear in Alexandria's awareness during Chapter 1. She may see him in passing — a gloved mercenary at the Factorage, a quiet man at the edge of her perception — but she does not register him as significant. He is background.

If Alexandria observes him without recognizing his significance, the prose must reflect this: she notes his hands (gloved — why?), his posture (coiled readiness, not slackness), his position relative to exits. These are tactical observations, not recognition. She does not know he is a mage. She does not know he is her father. She does not know he is relevant to her mission. He is a data point. She files him accordingly.

**What Hark Knows (for audit purposes — none of this enters Chapter 1):**

- He knows he has been in Salthaven for three years
- He knows Renne is in Salthaven — he has been avoiding her
- He knows the Order's methods intimately
- He does NOT know Alexandria exists
- He does NOT know the new junior escort at House Kellan is his daughter

## The Mission Parameters (as Alexandria Understands Them)

- **Official:** Routine long-term surveillance. Map unmarked magical activity in Salthaven and surrounding islands.
- **Cover:** Junior escort-in-training for House Kellan. This provides legitimate reason to travel, observe, and ask questions.
- **Reporting:** Reports go to Vara at the scribe's shop. Vara forwards to regional Order branch.

## Sensory and Perceptual Constraints — Chapter 1

Alexandria's perception in Chapter 1 is governed by her training and her nature:

**What She Notices First:**
- Hands — are they empty, gloved, twitching, too still?
- Exits — doors, windows, alleys, gaps in a crowd
- Weapons — the shift of a coat over a holster, the weight of her own blade
- Faces — reluctantly; micro-expressions of threat or contempt
- Small beautiful things — a clean line of light, a child's unguarded laugh; noticed and immediately suppressed

**What She Does NOT Notice (or notices and suppresses):**
- The emotional temperature of a room
- Kindness that is not tactical
- Beauty that is not useful
- Her own loneliness (she has no word for it)
- Her own hunger for gentleness (she has no framework for it)

**Her Sensory Filter:**
- She processes the world as a threat matrix
- Every environment is exits, cover, hostiles, assets
- People are potential threats, potential tools, or irrelevant
- She does not know she is doing this; it is simply how she perceives

**Her Internal Vocabulary Constraints:**
- She does not have words for: loneliness, gentleness, love, trust, want, afraid
- Her internal monologue is procedural, taxonomic, tactical
- She classifies; she does not feel (or rather, she feels but does not register feeling as a category of information)
- When she experiences an emotion she cannot name, it manifests as a somatic cue she files under *anomalous physical response* or ignores entirely

## Specific Prose Constraints — Chapter 1

**Alexandria must NOT:**
- Name her own emotional state (*she felt lonely, she was afraid, she wanted*)
- Use metaphors drawn from experiences she has not had (family, love, home, childhood)
- Reflect on her own psychology with any meta-awareness (*she knew she was suppressing something*)
- Use the word *mother, father, daughter, family* with any personal referent
- Use the word *want* for anything beyond tactical preference (*she wanted a better angle* is permitted; *she wanted to be held* is not)
- Notice Hark or Renne as significant — they are not in her threat matrix yet
- Know anything about the network, the River, or the healer with bandaged hands

**Alexandria MAY:**
- Catalog hands, exits, weapons, faces
- Note anomalies (*the harbormaster's manifest was incomplete; file for later*)
- Register somatic cues without naming the emotion behind them (*her chest tightened* — no annotation of why)
- Use tactical vocabulary (*threat assessment, situational awareness, mission parameters*)
- Observe the town with clinical precision
- Feel without knowing she is feeling
