Key Knowledge Shifts Before Chapter 20

· Alexandria has experienced two devastating revelations: Hark's abandonment and Renne's identity as her mother. She is emotionally shattered but physically steadied by the sparring session with Jera and Lennis. She is now poised to confront Vara, the one person who can bridge the gap between the files she's read and the truth she's living.
· Vara is in a precarious position. She helped Alexandria access the foundling record, knowing what it would reveal. She watched Alexandria's reaction. She knows Alexandria is now compromised beyond recovery. The confrontation in Chapter 20 will force Vara to explicitly choose a side—and she will choose Alexandria.
· Hark and Renne are now aligned and aware of each other's connection to Alexandria, but neither knows that Alexandria has just discovered half the truth on her own. They are making plans to protect her, unaware that she is currently in the dark about their alliance.
· The dramatic irony is shifting. The parents know about each other and about Alexandria. Alexandria knows about her mother but not her father. The gap will close when Alexandria confronts Renne (Chapter 31) or when Renne or Hark tells her the full truth during the crisis. Before that, Vara's confession in Chapter 20 will provide Alexandria with a crucial ally who knows more than she's been saying.
