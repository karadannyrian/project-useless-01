- **Hark** has made a decision that fractures his relationship with Alexandria. He is now physically and emotionally at his lowest, having retreated into the hollow shell of his old rules. His request to Vara creates a new, explicit alliance.

- **The Order** remains blind, but the gap between Alexandria's mission and her actions is now an active chasm. The dramatic irony continues to build.
