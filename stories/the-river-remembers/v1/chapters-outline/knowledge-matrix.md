### Key Knowledge Shifts Before Chapter 18

- **Hark** has been primed by Kel's confrontation. He knows Renne has been grieving him, that someone fiercely protective is watching, and that he has a history of hurting her. Now she's at his door. He has no idea why.
- **Renne** is about to speak words she's held for seventeen years. She knows Hark almost died for their daughter without knowing who she was. She is terrified but resolved.
- **Kel** has chosen to let the meeting happen without interference. He knows Renne is at the ropewalk but doesn't know the content of the conversation. He is waiting.
- **Alexandria** remains completely ignorant that her parents are about to share the secret of her existence. The dramatic irony is at its peak.
- **The Order** remains blind to all of this. The intelligence gap between what they know and what is happening on the ground is now a chasm.
